Anand Mohankumar
Security and Compliance Professional
I help organizations build trust by connecting cybersecurity, compliance, and business operations. With over three years of hands-on experience in Security Operations, I've investigated incidents, managed vulnerabilities, and strengthened defenses that protect organizational assets. My background in EQS SecOps gives me a deep technical understanding of how threats emerge — and how to contain them quickly.
Currently, as a Revenue Operations Analyst at EQS Group, I bridge the gap between security assurance and business enablement. I manage Due Diligence, and regulatory compliance initiatives, ensuring that every customer interaction aligns with our security and data protection standards. This cross-functional exposure has deepened my expertise in TPRM, ISMS frameworks, and product security governance.
I'm passionate about uniting technical defense with organizational resilience — helping teams move beyond just detecting threats to building secure systems that earn customer confidence.
